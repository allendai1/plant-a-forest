# M4. Targeting and planting

Status: **done 2026-10-06.**

## 1. Goal

From [docs/MVP_PLAN.md](../MVP_PLAN.md): the client picks the closest open hex in the open ring within planting range (PC hover and mobile tap can pick another hex in range), shows a target indicator (outline plus a "12 / 40" meter, gold when the next press finishes the tree), and shows an arrow to the nearest open plot when carrying seeds with nothing in range. The plant remote sends the hex; the server validates everything. Each press plants `BulkPlant` seeds, overflow goes to the next-closest open hex, and holding E repeats. The first seed records the starter UserId, and rings open outside-in.

Done when: two clients can plant into the same tree, and a spoofed remote (wrong distance, locked ring, no seeds, full hex, spam) is rejected.

## 2. Design check

- **Plant where you stand:** "the open hex closest to you within your planting range ... On PC you can point at a different hex in range instead." **Change for mobile (decided):** no picking. The nearest open tile is selected automatically, and **a tap anywhere on the game view plants into it** (holding the plant button repeats). Players just want to see the numbers go up, so mobile shouldn't need precision. Distances are measured flat (ignoring height) from the character to the hex center. The range is `BaseRangeStuds` (30) plus the Planting Range upgrade (level 0 until M7). Neighboring hexes are 27.7 studs apart, so from any tile you can reach your own and its neighbors.
- **Open hex:** in the open ring (`ring == UnlockedRing`) and not yet full (seeds < 40). Empty or started, it doesn't matter: "Anyone's seeds can grow any unfinished tree."
- **Overflow:** "If a press would overfill that tree, the extra seeds go into the next-closest open hex." The server picks it: the next-closest open hex to the player, within range (question 1).
- **One press, one seed; holding repeats:** a press plants `min(BulkPlant value, carried)`, which is 1 until M7. Holding E (or the gamepad button, or the mobile plant button) repeats every `HoldRepeatSeconds`.
- **Rings close in:** "The next ring inward opens only when every tree in the current ring is full grown." When the last plot of the open ring fills, `UnlockedRing` drops by one. When ring 5 fills, the forest is complete; the Awakening comes in M6, so M4 just logs it.
- **Target indicator, local only:** "a bright hex-shaped outline and a small floating meter ... The outline turns gold when your next press will finish the tree ... If no open hex is in range, nothing is highlighted and the plant button dims."
- **Arrow:** "Whenever you're carrying seeds and no open plot is in range, a glowing arrow points to the nearest one."

## 4. Remotes and data

- **`PlantSeeds`** (C→S, `q: number, r: number`), as in ARCHITECTURE section 5. Server checks, in order:
  1. Rate limit: 12 per second, bursts of 4.
  2. `q` and `r` are finite whole numbers inside the grid.
  3. The plot exists.
  4. It's in the open ring and not full.
  5. The player is alive and within range + 4 studs of it (flat distance, using the server's own copy of their position).
  6. They carry at least 1 seed.

  The server then plants: the target first, overflow to the next-closest open plots in range.
- **`HexBatch`** (S→C, all players): from ARCHITECTURE section 6. It was planned for M5, but M4 needs it now so the meter and rims update as anyone plants. That's a change to the milestone order, not to the design.
- **Attributes:** `Seeds` (updated at each flush) and `UnlockedRing` on `ForestState`.
- **Saved data:** none.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Overflow** (only possible once Bulk Plant plants more than 1 per press) goes to the open hex closest to the player.
2. **Walkable tiles:** the hexagon tiles get collision so players walk on top of them.
3. **Planting Range upgrade:** +4 studs per level (0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20), to be tested. Spreadsheet: Planting Range values 0/4/8/12/16/20.
4. **Mobile:** the nearest open tile is selected automatically, and a tap anywhere on the game view plants into it. There's no tap-to-pick. A drag (moving the camera) isn't a tap, so it doesn't plant.

## 6. Test plan

1. **Static checks** pass, plus `tests/TargetingCheck.luau` in Studio.
2. **Planting:** `setCarried 50`, walk to ring 15. The outline and "0 / 40" meter appear on the closest open plot. Each press plants 1: the meter goes 1, 2, 3 ..., `Carried` drops, and the starter UserId is recorded on the first seed. Holding E plants every 0.12 s. At 39 / 40 the outline is gold.
3. **Overflow and full:** with a plot at 39 / 40 and `setBulkPlant 5`, one press fills it and the rest go to the next-closest open plot. A full plot loses its rim and can't be targeted.
4. **Ring unlock:** `fillOpenRing 1` leaves one plot of ring 15. Filling it opens ring 14: the rims move inward and `UnlockedRing` becomes 14.
5. **Arrow:** with seeds in the hub (nothing in range), the arrow and marker point to the nearest open plot. They disappear once a plot is in range.
6. **Prompt priority:** next to the rack, E grabs (the prompt wins); in the forest, E plants.
6b. **Touch:** with the touch screen emulated, a tap on the game view plants into the outlined tile, and a camera drag doesn't.
7. **Exploits** (fired straight at the remote from the client), all rejected with nothing planted:
   - a plot 200 studs away
   - a plot in a locked ring
   - planting with 0 seeds
   - a full plot
   - NaN, 1.5, `"3"`, `math.huge` and out-of-grid coordinates
   - 50 requests in one burst (only the rate limit's worth go through)
8. **Two players (your test: Test tab → Clients and Servers → 2 players):** both plant into the same plot; each sees the meter count both players' seeds, and the starter stays the first player.

## What changed from the plan

- **Targeting rounds distances to a thousandth of a stud.** Roblox vectors are 32-bit, so hexes exactly the same distance away came out a few millionths apart and the q-then-r tie-break never applied. `tests/TargetingCheck.luau` caught it.
- **`tests/TargetingCheck.luau` runs straight from the repo:** Studio fetches it from the local server and runs it with `loadstring`.
- **No other changes.** The meter sits 7 studs above the target and is drawn on top of everything. Note that Studio's screen capture leaves out always-on-top labels, so it can't be seen in screenshots.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection):**
- `tests/TargetingCheck.luau`: all checks pass (closest first, the six neighbors in q-then-r order, range edges at ±0.01 studs, full hexes skipped, height ignored, 13 hexes in 50 studs and all 19 in 56).
- Startup: 6 services, 5 controllers, no errors from game code. The client built 594 tiles, 84 open.
- **Walkable tiles:** the character stands at y 4.5 on a tile (tile top 1.6), not on the floor.
- **Planting** (the same `PlantSeeds` requests pressing E sends):
  - The outline sat on the closest open plot. From a locked ring-14 tile it targeted the neighboring ring-15 plot, the closest open one.
  - Requests every 0.12 s planted 1 seed each, and the meter followed.
  - The first seed recorded the starter UserId and a species on every planted plot.
  - The server's plot totals (111 seeds) matched the Forest Bar's `Seeds` attribute.
- **Full plot:** a plot at 40 lost its rim (84 → 83 rims).
- **Ring unlock:** filling the rest of ring 15 set `UnlockedRing` to 14, and the rims moved to ring 14's 78 plots.
- **Overflow:** with 45 seeds per press (`setBulkPlant 45`), one press into an empty ring-14 plot put 40 in it and 5 into (9,5). That's the closest open plot to the player, a neighbor at 27.7 studs, chosen over the equally close (11,3) by the q-then-r order.
- **Arrow:** in the hub with seeds, the arrow and marker pointed at the nearest open plot. Once in range they disappeared and the outline showed.
- **Rejected:** with 0 seeds, nothing planted. The plot on the far side of the map and the locked ring-14 neighbor both stayed at 0, and no ring-14 plot had any seeds until ring 14 opened. NaN, 11.5, "11", infinity, (99, 0), a missing argument and no arguments planted nothing that could be traced. 50 requests at once didn't plant faster than the rate limit allows (+5).
- Selene, StyLua and luau-lsp pass. All 20 scripts in Studio match the disk byte for byte.

**Testing note:** someone was moving the character and planting with E in the Studio window during the tests. It showed up as movement input and plants I hadn't sent, which **confirms that pressing E plants for real** (it planted 15 seeds). It also added background planting to some measurements, so those results come from checking specific plots in server snapshots instead of the forest total.

**Not verified:**

- That E goes to the rack or bench prompt instead of planting when both are possible. That only happens once ring 5 opens next to the hub.
- **Two players planting into the same tree** (Test tab → Clients and Servers → 2 players).

**Place changes to save:** the synced scripts. There's no map change; the plots planted during testing reset when Play stops.

## Fix after M4 (2026-10-06)

- **Planting could keep draining on its own.** If a rack or bench prompt appeared while E was held, the release went to the prompt before the hold loop was stopped, so planting kept repeating whenever an open plot came into range. The release now always stops the loop, and losing window focus stops it too.
- **Left mouse plants** on PC: a click plants one seed, holding repeats, the same as E.
- **Verified in Studio with real input:** no input for 3 s planted nothing; 3 clicks planted 3; holding the mouse 1.2 s planted 12 and stopped on release; holding E, moving to the rack (prompt shown), releasing there and coming back planted nothing over 3 s.
- **Not verified:** that a tap on a touch screen doesn't count as a left click (it shouldn't; touch has its own input type).
