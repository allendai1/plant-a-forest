# M3. Seed rack and carrying

Status: **done 2026-10-06.**

## 1. Goal

From `docs/MVP_PLAN.md`: a seed rack prompt in the hub where each press grabs `BulkPickup` seeds (1 by default) up to capacity, with holding E repeating every `HoldRepeatSeconds`; a giant seed placeholder over the head, scaled by the number of seeds carried (using the `GiantSeedMilestones` thresholds), with a count label; the carried count lives on the server.

Done when: one player can fill to capacity by holding E, and the giant seed visibly grows at 5 and 15 seeds.

## 2. Design check

- **The rack:** "only the free, unlimited seed rack in the hub." A pile of giant acorns with a "FREE SEEDS" sign, in the next sector over from the spawn: about 88 studs out at 330°, roughly 108 studs (about 3.4 s at the starting walk speed) from the spawn, across one walkway.
- **One press, one seed; holding repeats:** "each press grabs or plants one seed at first, and holding E repeats. The Bulk Pickup and Bulk Plant upgrades raise how many seeds move per press." Each grab adds `min(BulkPickup value, capacity − carried)`. Bulk Pickup is level 0 until M7, so 1 seed per grab.
- **Capacity:** `GameConfig.capacity(Strength)`, from M2. A full player can't grab more.
- **The giant seed:** "You carry your seeds as one huge seed held overhead, with a number above it showing how many it holds ... The giant seed's look follows how many seeds you're holding right now." Every client draws it over **every** player's head from their `Carried` attribute (ARCHITECTURE section 4), so you can see what others carry.
- **Carried seeds are session-only:** decided earlier. They're kept on the server per player (not per character), so they survive dying, and lost on leaving.

## 4. Remotes and data

- **Change to ARCHITECTURE:** `GrabSeeds` (C→S, fired by the client on every press and repeat) is replaced by the rack's ProximityPrompt, like the benches in M2b. Its `Triggered` and `TriggerEnded` events fire on the server, so the server runs the hold-repeat itself. There's no remote to spam, mobile gets a tap button for free, and the same E key works the same way at the rack and the benches. The checks are the same as planned for `GrabSeeds`: alive, in reach, not full, rate-limited.
- **Player attribute `Carried`** (ARCHITECTURE section 6): a display copy. The server keeps its own count.
- **Saved data:** none (carried seeds are session-only).

## 5. Decisions

1. **Seed size steps at milestones**, with a pop, staying the same size in between.
2. **Sizes:** 1.6 / 5 / 10 / 16 / 28 / 45 / 70 studs for 1 / 5 / 15 / 40 / 80 / 150 / 250 seeds.
3. **The acorn model:** placeholder parts now, a Blender acorn in the art pass.
4. **Rack location:** further from the spawn than first proposed: about 88 studs out at 330°, the next sector over (about 108 studs, 3.4 s, from the spawn).

## 6. Test plan

1. **Static checks:** Selene, StyLua and luau-lsp pass.
2. **Done when:** set Strength so capacity is 20, hold E at the rack: `Carried` climbs by 1 every 0.12 s and stops at exactly 20. At 0 Strength (capacity 1) it stops at 1.
3. **Seed size:** the giant seed appears at 1 seed (1.6 studs), grows at 5 (5 studs) and at 15 (10 studs), with the label matching `Carried`.
4. **Stopping:** releasing E stops grabbing; walking out of range stops it too; being full stops it and shows "Full! Go plant."
5. **Respawn:** carried seeds survive dying, and the seed reappears on the new character.
6. **Exploits:** the client setting its own `Carried` to 999 changes nothing on the server. Rapid press/release cycles grab no faster than one per 0.12 s. Triggering the rack from far away (simulated) is refused.
7. **Two players (your test: Test tab → Clients and Servers → 2 players):** each sees the other's giant seed and number.

## What changed from the plan

- **Nothing in the design.** Small notes:
  - Holding fills at about 0.13 s per seed instead of 0.12. The server's repeat wakes on the next frame (60 per second), which adds about a hundredth of a second. It's tunable through `HoldRepeatSeconds`.
  - The ARCHITECTURE notes for CarryService and CarryController now describe the prompt-based rack. The planned `MaxGrabRequestsPerSecond` isn't needed, because the minimum gap between grabs does that job.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection):**
- Startup: 6 services, 4 controllers, no errors from game code.
- Capacity 1 (0 Strength): holding E grabs exactly 1 seed, then nothing more.
- **Done when:** at capacity 20 (Strength 475), holding E took `Carried` from 0 to exactly 20 in 2.58 s (about 0.13 s per seed) and stopped. The giant seed was 1.6 studs at 1 to 4 seeds, 5 studs at 5 to 14, and 10 studs at 15 and above. The label matched the count, and the HUD showed "Seeds 20 / 20" and "Full! Go plant."
- Releasing E stopped grabbing at once; walking 30 studs away while holding stopped it; 30 taps in 0.5 s gave +5, no faster than holding.
- The client setting its own `Carried` to 999 only changed that client's screen. The server kept 14, and the next grab overwrote the fake with 15.
- Dying kept all 14 seeds, and the seed reattached to the new head.
- Selene, StyLua and luau-lsp pass. All 17 scripts in Studio match the disk byte for byte.

**Not verified:**
- **Two players** (Test tab → Clients and Servers → 2 players): each should see the other's giant seed and number.
- **Mobile tap:** holding the on-screen prompt button should repeat the same way.

**Note on testing:** the first simulated press after teleporting the character next to the rack is refused, because the server still has the old position for a moment. Real players walk up, so this can't happen in play.
