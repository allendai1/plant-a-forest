# M7. Upgrades

Status: **done 2026-10-06.**

## 1. Goal

From `docs/MVP_PLAN.md`: the upgrade shop for Bulk Pickup, Bulk Plant and Planting Range (Quick Hands is dropped from the MVP), with costs from `GameConfig.upgradeCost` and every purchase checked on the server.

Done when: buying each level takes the right number of coins and changes how the game plays.

## 2. What the player gets

The shop opens when you stand in the glowing ring in front of the altar (built in M6b). It becomes a real shop: each upgrade shows its level, what the next level does, its price and a Buy button.

| Upgrade | What it does | Levels (per press / range) | Cost of each level (coins) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bulk Pickup** | Seeds you grab per press at a rack (holding E repeats) | 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → **all you can carry** | 50, 200, 800, 3,200, 12,800 |
| **Bulk Plant** | Seeds you plant per press (holding E or the mouse repeats) | 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → **all you carry** | 50, 200, 800, 3,200, 12,800 |
| **Planting Range** | How far away you can plant from | 30 studs → 34 → 38 → 42 → 46 → 50 | 60, 180, 540, 1,620, 4,860 |

These are the numbers already in `GameConfig.Upgrades`, unchanged. For scale: a player earns 1 coin per seed planted plus 500 for each finished forest. With about 30 players, a forest is roughly 800 seeds each, so about 1,300 coins per forest. The first levels come within minutes, and the last ones take about 10 forests.

Rules from the GDD that still apply:
- "One press moves one seed ... Bulk Pickup and Bulk Plant raise the per-press amount."
- With Bulk Plant, extra seeds that overfill a tree go to the next-closest open plot in range (already built in M4).
- The server decides everything. The client only asks "buy Bulk Plant".

## 4. Remotes and data

- **`BuyUpgrade`** (client → server, `upgradeId: string`), already listed in ARCHITECTURE and created since M0. Server checks, in order:
  1. rate limit (4 per second)
  2. the id is a string and one of the three shop upgrades
  3. the level is below the maximum
  4. the player has at least the price in coins
  5. coins are taken and the level goes up in the same step, with nothing in between that could let two purchases overlap
- **Attributes:** `BulkPickupLevel`, `BulkPlantLevel`, `PlantingRangeLevel` on each player (display copies; the server reads its own profile).
- **Saved data:** none yet. Coins and levels are saved in M8.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Prices and values:** as in the table above, to be retuned after a playtest.
2. **Buying only at the shop:** yes. The server refuses a purchase unless the player is standing in the shop ring (with 4 studs of slack for lag).
3. **Top level "all":** yes, for now.
4. **Robux items in the shop:** later, with the rest of monetization.

## 6. Test plan

1. **Static checks** (StyLua, Selene, luau-lsp) and the byte-for-byte sync check.
2. **Buying (Done when, part 1):** set 25,000 coins, then buy every level of all three upgrades with real mouse clicks in the shop. After each purchase, coins drop by exactly that level's price and the level goes up by one. At level 5 the button reads MAX and does nothing. With too few coins the button is dimmed and the server refuses.
3. **Behavior (Done when, part 2):**
   - **Bulk Pickup:** one E press at a rack adds exactly 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 seeds at levels 0 to 4, and fills you to capacity at level 5.
   - **Bulk Plant:** one press plants exactly that many into the target, with overflow into the next plot.
   - **Planting Range:** from 45 studs away, an open plot isn't targeted (and the server refuses it) at level 0, but is targeted and accepted at level 4 (46 studs).
4. **Exploits:** `BuyUpgrade` fired directly with a made-up id, a number, `nil`, an upgrade already at max, not enough coins, and 30 requests at once. Nothing is bought beyond the rate limit and what the player can afford, and coins never go negative.

## What changed from the plan

- **The server checks you're at the shop** (decision 2): `EconomyService` refuses `BuyUpgrade` unless the player's character is inside the `ShopZone` ring (+4 studs). The ring is the same one that opens the panel.
- **Test commands:** `setCoins`, `setBulkPickupLevel`, `setBulkPlantLevel` and `setPlantingRangeLevel` replace M4's `setBulkPlant`. The test panel has a "+1,000 coins" button.
- **The gold "this press finishes the tree" outline** now counts what one press really plants: your Bulk Plant amount, capped by the seeds you carry.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection):**
- **Buying with real mouse clicks in the shop**, starting from 25,000 coins. Every purchase took exactly that level's price:
  - Bulk Pickup 50, 200, 800, 3,200, 12,800 up to level 5.
  - Bulk Plant 50, 200, 800, 3,200 up to level 4.
  - Planting Range 60, 180, 540, 1,620 up to level 4.

  Each raised the level by one. Clicking at MAX did nothing, and the next levels I couldn't afford (12,800 and 4,860, with 1,300 coins left) were dimmed and bought nothing. The panel showed "Level 4 / 5 · 46 studs → 50 studs | 4,860 coins".
- **Bulk Pickup** (real E presses at a rack): one press grabbed 4 at level 2, 16 at level 4, and filled the player to capacity (245) at level 5.
- **Bulk Plant:** one press planted 8 at level 3. At level 5 one press planted 80 of 92: it filled the target tree, overflowed into the two open neighbours in range, and kept 12 because no other open plot was in reach.
- **Planting Range:** from 45 studs away, an open plot was neither targeted nor accepted at level 0, and was both at level 4 (46 studs).
- **Exploits** (`BuyUpgrade` fired directly):
  - away from the shop: nothing bought
  - made-up ids, numbers, nil, a 1,000-character string: nothing bought
  - 30 requests at once: 4 went through (the burst limit), each charged its normal price
  - not enough coins: nothing bought

  Coins never went negative.
- StyLua, Selene and luau-lsp pass.

**Not verified:** two players buying at once, and the shop on a phone.
