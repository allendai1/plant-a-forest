# M5. Growth, Forest Bar and coins

Status: **done 2026-10-06.** Approved with the recommendations below; the starter's name label was postponed.

## 1. Goal

From `docs/MVP_PLAN.md`: tree models (leafy and pine, 4 stages each) picked by `GameConfig.growthStage`; tile states (open tiles pulse, started tiles mossy with a glowing rim, finished tiles mossy); on every seed a squash-and-stretch bounce and +1 coin to the planter; on the finishing seed a leaf burst and a chime placeholder; the ring complete moment; the Forest Bar on the HUD (seeds / target); the Ancient Tree growing with the bar. (The starter's name near a tree was postponed.)

Done when: a full trip shows bounces on every seed and a stage swap at every stage boundary, finishing a ring plays the ring complete moment, and the bar and the Ancient Tree match the server count.

## 2. Design check

- **Instant, staged growth:** "Every seed counts the moment it touches a plot: the Forest Bar ticks up and a coin pops out." Stages from `GameConfig.growthStage(seeds, 40)`: sprout 1–10, sapling 11–20, young 21–39, full grown 40. Models are `Assets.Trees.<Leafy|Pine><stage>` (2 MeshParts each: bark and leaves/needles), species from the plot's `species` (`Trees.Species`).
- **Natural placement:** "Each tree sits slightly off its tile's center with random rotation and size." `Random.new(treeSeed)` gives every client the same offset (up to `TreeJitterFraction` × HexSize = 4 studs), yaw and a size factor (0.9–1.1).
- **Bounce per seed:** "every seed makes the tree squash down and spring back up, ending slightly bigger." Within a stage the tree scales from 80% to 100% of its model as seeds come in, so each seed ends a little bigger, and the next stage's model starts at 80%. The bounce is `Growth.BounceSeconds` (0.25 s): squash (wider, shorter), stretch, settle.
- **Finishing pop:** "a burst of leaves, a short chime, and the tree settles into its final pose."
- **Tile states:** "locked tiles are dark; open, empty tiles are bright with a glowing rim and a soft pulse; started tiles turn mossy and keep the glowing rim until the tree is full grown; finished tiles are mossy under their tree."
- **Coins:** "1 coin (times mutation) goes to the player who delivered it." Mutations come in M9 (multiplier 1 now) and the Awakening boost in M6. Coins are session-only until M8 saves them.
- **Ring complete moment:** the sequence from `docs/DESIGN_CHANGES.md` (wave with sway and bounce, golden sweep, leaf burst and rising chime, the next ring's rims lighting up, the Ancient Tree shaking, the bar pulsing, banner "Ring 3 of 11 complete!"). Rings count in the order they're planted, so ring 15 is "Ring 1 of 11". Client-side only, triggered by `UnlockedRing` dropping.
- **Ancient Tree:** "grows as the Forest Bar fills, from a sapling at the start of a forest to a giant ... at 100%", 4 models "and scales smoothly between them as the bar fills". `AncientBase` never scales.

## 4. Remotes and data

- **No new remotes.** Coins arrive by the `Coins` attribute, the bar by `Seeds` / `Target`, the ring moment by `UnlockedRing`, all already planned in ARCHITECTURE section 6.
- **New player attribute:** `Coins` (listed in ARCHITECTURE section 6).
- **Saved data:** none yet (`Coins` is in the profile shape but only in memory until M8).
- **Deviation:** coins live in DataService for now instead of a new EconomyService, because M5 only adds coins and there's nothing else for EconomyService to own until M7's shop. `DataService.add(player, "Coins", n)` is the same call EconomyService will make.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Solid trunks:** trunks become solid **only at full grown**. A growing trunk that suddenly gets bigger would shove the player standing on the tile, and nobody needs to stand on a finished plot. Leaves never collide. The Ancient Tree's bark is solid at every stage (the hub keeps clear of its roots). The alternative is trees you always walk through.
2. **Ancient Tree growth:** equal quarters of the bar: 0–25% shows Ancient1 growing from 30% to 100% of its size, 25–50% Ancient2 from 44% (Ancient1's height) to 100%, and so on, so a model swap is never a visible jump. At 0% it's a ~16-stud sapling, at 100% the full 354 studs. The alternative is one step per completed ring (11 steps), which matches "grows a step whenever a ring is completed" but sits still for minutes at a time. With my recommendation the ring moment adds only a shake.
3. **Coin display:** a coin counter under the Forest Bar with a "+N" that floats up beside it (merged, so holding E doesn't spam it). No coin pop over the tree.
4. **One bounce per batch per tree,** with a bigger squash when several seeds arrived together.
5. **Starter's name label:** postponed (not in M5). The starter UserId is still stored per plot.

## What changed from the plan

- **The starter's name label was left out** (your call at approval). `GameConfig` is unchanged as a result: no new balance numbers, so nothing to update in `docs/Balancing.xlsx`.
- **ForestController hands the Ancient Tree its ground height** (`AncientTreeController.build(...)`, from the plots' server-raycast heights) instead of the two controllers requiring each other, which Luau doesn't allow.
- **HUD layout:** the Forest Bar and coins sit at the top center, the "Full!" hint below them, and the stats label moved down to y 112 so it never overlaps the bar on narrow screens.
- **Placeholder effects:** the leaf burst uses Roblox's default particle texture tinted green, and the chime is the built-in `electronicpingshort.wav`, played at 1, 1.26 and 1.5 speed for the ring's rising chime.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection):**
- **A full tree:** 40 `PlantSeeds` requests into one empty ring-15 plot. Every seed made the tree squash and stretch (its trunk's height-to-width ratio varied about 20% in each 0.4 s after a seed). The model went Pine1 for seeds 1–10, Pine2 for 11–20, Pine3 for 21–39 and Pine4 at 40. Coins went 0 → 40, one per seed, and the HUD showed them. The finished tile was mossy with tufts and no rim (screenshot).
- **Ring moment:** `fillOpenRing 1`, then finishing the last plot of ring 15. `UnlockedRing` went 15 → 14. The gold sweep ran across the tiles for 2 s, then ring 14's 78 rims lit up over 0.6 s (5, 20, 35, 50, 65, 78). The banner read "Ring 1 of 11 complete!" and the Ancient Tree tilted up to 1.1° while shaking.
- **Bar and Ancient Tree:** the HUD text matched the server's `Seeds` / `Target` at 14%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 99% (`fillForest`). The Ancient Tree showed the expected model at the expected height every time (Ancient1 38.1, Ancient2 54.7, Ancient3 123.6, Ancient4 230.4 and 349.0 studs). Each model takes over at exactly the previous one's full height, so there's no jump.
- **Trunks:** at 99%, all 588 full-grown trunks were solid, the one sprout's trunk wasn't, and no leaves were.
- **Performance:** at 99%, 589 trees and 2,368 parts in `ForestView`, 60 fps in Studio.
- StyLua, Selene and luau-lsp pass. The six synced scripts match the disk byte for byte.

**Not verified:**
