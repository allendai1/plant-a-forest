# M2. Stats and training

Status: **done 2026-10-06** (approved with option A for the hub, per-player rep timers, and the pyramid's measured rate).

## 1. Goal

From `docs/MVP_PLAN.md`: Strength and Speed stored on the server per player; bench press and treadmill zones give +`TrainingRatePerSecond` × gym multiplier each second while standing in them (server tick, no client input); Humanoid WalkSpeed = `GameConfig.walkSpeed(Speed)`; capacity = `GameConfig.capacity(Strength)`.

Done when (updated for the pyramid's measured rate): standing on the bench press for 1 second (4 reps) raises capacity from 1 to 2, 7 seconds gives 28 Strength (carry 4), and walk speed is 32 at 0 Speed and about 46 at 230.

## 2. Design check

- **Training is automatic:** "Stand on the bench press or treadmill and the stat goes up on its own (ticked by the server, not by clicks). No tapping is needed, and AFK training is allowed. Planting never raises stats."
- **Rate, measured in Build the Pyramid:** about 10 points in 2.5 seconds at 1x, and 100 Strength in 13 seconds at 2x with +2 per rep. That's **a rep every 0.25 seconds** (4 per second), and the gym multiplier makes each rep worth more: **+1 × multiplier per rep**. The GDD said +1 per second, which is 4 times slower, so the GDD and the done-when line are updated.
- **Per-player timer:** the server checks who is standing in a `TrainingZone` part every 0.05 seconds and adds up how long each player has been on their current station. Every full 0.25 seconds on it pays one rep of that station's stat. Stepping off (or switching stations) throws away the unfinished rep. There's no remote for training at all, so there's nothing for a client to spoof.
- **Gym multiplier:** "2x after 1 forest, 5x after 3, 10x after 8, 25x after 15", via `GameConfig.gymMultiplier`. ForestsCompleted stays 0 until M6, so M2 always trains at 1x; a Studio test command checks the other steps.
- **Walk speed:** "Players start at 32 studs per second ... reach 46 at 230 Speed." The server sets `Humanoid.WalkSpeed = walkSpeed(Speed)` on every spawn and whenever Speed changes.
- **Capacity:** `capacity(Strength)`. Nothing uses it until M3 (the rack), so in M2 it's only computed for the HUD readout. The client computes it from the replicated `Strength` attribute, as ARCHITECTURE section 6 says (capacity isn't replicated separately).
- **Camera** (from the redesign): every player gets Invisicam and a max zoom of 60 studs on join, applied by the server per ARCHITECTURE section 3. This moves the setting out of the place and into code, as planned in M1.

## 4. Remotes and data

- **`DevCommand`:** the first use of the Studio-only remote from ARCHITECTURE section 5, with the same payload and checks. It's created by DevService, not by `Remotes.createAll()`, so live servers never have it.
- **No other remotes.** Training has none by design.
- **Player attributes (ARCHITECTURE section 6):** `Strength`, `Speed`, `ForestsCompleted`. They're display copies; the server reads its own profile table.
- **Saved data:** none yet. The profile is in memory until M8. Its three fields match the saved profile in ARCHITECTURE section 7.
- No changes to ARCHITECTURE.md.

## 5. Decisions and open questions

1. **Bigger hub:** decided. The hub grows by one ring so the stations fit between the world tree's roots and the forest. Proposed option A keeps the map's outer edge: forest rings 5–15, 594 plots, 40 seeds per tree, target 23,760, walk times unchanged. (Option B would push the forest out a ring: 684 plots, 35 seeds, about 2.5 s longer to the outer ring.)
2. **Per-player timer:** decided. Every full rep interval (0.25 s) on a station pays one rep, counted from the moment the player steps on, so 7 seconds gives exactly 28 at 1x. Stepping off throws away the unfinished rep. Bench press → Strength, treadmill → Speed, as in the GDD.
3. **Training rate:** decided from the pyramid measurements: a rep every 0.25 s worth +1 × gym multiplier.

## 6. Test plan

1. **Static checks:** Selene, StyLua and luau-lsp pass.
2. **Grid after the bigger hub:** the server prints 594 plots and target 23,760, with per-ring counts from 24 (ring 5) to 84 (ring 15). The client builds 594 tiles, with 84 open.
3. **Done when, bench:** in a playtest, walk the character onto the bench press (Studio's character navigation tool) and stand for 7 seconds. `Strength` reaches 4 after about 1 second (Carry 1 → 2 on the HUD) and 28 after 7 seconds (Carry 4). Step off: Strength stops rising. Step off at 0.6 seconds and back on: the unfinished rep is dropped and the count restarts.
4. **Done when, walk speed:** at 0 Speed, `Humanoid.WalkSpeed` is 32. Stand on the treadmill and watch it rise each tick. `setSpeed 230` → WalkSpeed 46.0 (formula: 32 + 28 × 230 / 460).
5. **Respawn:** reset the character with Speed 230; WalkSpeed is 46 again on the new character.
6. **Multiplier:** `setForestsCompleted 1` → +2 per rep (about 100 Strength in 12.5 seconds, matching the pyramid's 13); `3` → +5 per rep.
7. **Camera:** the player's `DevCameraOcclusionMode` is Invisicam and `CameraMaxZoomDistance` is 60.
8. **Exploit:** the client sets its own `Strength` attribute to 1,000,000 locally. The server's value is unchanged and the next tick still adds 1 from the real value. `DevCommand` with an unknown command, NaN, a negative number or 1e12 is ignored.
9. **Multi-client (2 players, Studio's local server):** both train at once on different stations; each only gains their own stat.

## What changed from the plan

- **The interval a player steps on in counts toward their first rep.** The server only notices a player on a station at its next check (up to 0.05 s later). Counting that interval gives at most 0.05 s of extra credit, and makes the timer match "from the moment you step on": 4 reps at 1.00 s instead of 3.
- **`GameConfig.Dev`** holds the DevCommand limits (values 0 to 10,000,000; 5 commands per second, bursts of 10), so the numbers aren't inline in DevService.
- **`tools/studio_sync.py` writes UTF-8**, because the Windows console's default encoding garbled the HUD's `·` separators.
