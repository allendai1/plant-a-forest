# M10. First-time experience

Status: **done 2026-10-06** (without quests: postponed by the user).

## 1. Goal

From [docs/MVP_PLAN.md](../MVP_PLAN.md):
- an arrow plus one-line prompts: grab a seed, plant it, then (after 3 plants) go train
- a free gift popup (like, favorite, join group) worth 500 coins once
- a quest list

Done when: a new player plants their first seed within 20 seconds without reading anything long.

## 2. What a new player sees

From the GDD's "First-time experience": one glowing arrow and one short line at a time, and nothing ever blocks planting. The arrow is the blue scrolling beam from M6b, which already leads to the nearest open plot when you're carrying seeds.

| When | Prompt (one line, top-centre) | The blue arrow leads to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You've never planted and carry nothing | "Grab seeds at an acorn pile" | the nearest acorn pile (about 25 studs from the spawn) |
| You carry seeds but have never planted | "Plant them in a glowing tile" | the nearest open plot (as today) |
| After your first plant | (prompts fade) | the nearest open plot whenever you carry seeds and none is in reach (as today) |
| After 3 plants, until you've trained a little (Strength 10) | "Get stronger to carry more" | the 1x training pad |
| Then, once you have 50+ coins and no upgrades yet | "Spend coins on upgrades" (until your first purchase or 100 seeds planted) | the shop ring |
| After that | The quest list takes over (below) | as today |

The step comes from what you've actually done (seeds planted, Strength, upgrades), which is saved. So returning players skip whatever they've already done, and nothing extra needs saving for the tutorial.

**The free gift:** a gift button on the left of the screen until it's claimed. About 90 seconds into a new player's first visit, a small popup shows once: "Free gift! Like and favorite the game for 500 coins". It has a **Favorite** button (Roblox's own favorite prompt) and a **Claim 500 coins** button. As decided in ARCHITECTURE, there's no check that you really liked or favorited. It can be claimed once per player, ever.

**Quests:** a small list on the left under the stats, with progress ("Plant 100 seeds 37 / 100"). Each pays coins once, when it completes.

| Quest | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Plant 100 seeds | 150 coins |
| Help finish 10 trees | 200 coins |
| Reach 500 Strength | 250 coins |
| Complete a forest (be there when the bar fills) | 300 coins |

The GDD's fourth quest, "plant during a weather event", is replaced by "complete a forest", since weather is postponed (question 1).

## 4. Remotes and data

- **`ClaimFreeGift`** (client → server, no payload): rate limit; refused if already claimed; otherwise +500 coins and `FreeGiftClaimed = 1` in the same step. As planned in ARCHITECTURE.
- **`TutorialProgress` dropped:** nothing to send, the step is worked out from saved counters.
- **Saved (new fields):** `SeedsPlanted`, `TreesHelped`, `FreeGiftClaimed`, and `QuestPlantSeeds`, `QuestHelpTrees`, `QuestStrength`, `QuestForest` (0/1 each). Flat numbers like the rest of the profile, so they work with DataService's `get / set / add` and the attributes.

## 5. Decisions

1. **Quests are postponed** (the user's answer to questions 1 to 3). So the quest list, `QuestService` and the `TreesHelped` counter aren't built. M10 is the tutorial prompts and arrow plus the free gift.
2. **The free gift is like and favorite only:** there's no Roblox group yet. A "join group" button can be added once there is one.

## 6. Test plan

1. **Static checks** and the byte-for-byte sync check.
2. **New player (Done when):** `resetProfile`, rejoin, and follow the prompts like a player would:
   - The first prompt and the arrow point at the acorn pile.
   - After grabbing, they point at an open plot.
   - Time from spawn to the first seed planted, walking at the starting speed: target under 20 s (the outer row is about 290 studs, around 9 s of walking).
   - After 3 plants the arrow points to the 1x pad. After training to 10 Strength, and with 50 coins, it points to the shop. After an upgrade, the quests take over.
3. **Returning player:** rejoin mid-tutorial; it continues from the right step. A player who has done everything sees no tutorial.
4. **Quests:** each quest completes at its goal, pays exactly once (including after rejoining), and its progress survives rejoining. "Help finish 10 trees" counts all helpers of a finished tree.
5. **Free gift:**
   - The popup shows once, about 90 s in, for a new player.
   - Claiming gives 500 coins; claiming again (also fired directly at the remote, and after rejoining) gives nothing.
6. **Exploits:** `ClaimFreeGift` spammed; nothing else can complete quests from the client.

## What changed from the plan

- **No quests** (decision 1). The profile gained only `SeedsPlanted` and `FreeGiftClaimed`, and `ClaimFreeGift` is handled in EconomyService (there's no QuestService).
- **TutorialController owns its own UI** (the prompt line, the gift button and the popup) instead of adding them to HUD.
- **The arrow now goes through the fence gates:** the fence around the plaza only opens at the six trails. The first test showed a new player following the straight arrow from the spawn into the fence. Now, when you and the arrow's target are on different sides of the fence, it first leads to a point 8 studs past the gate on the shortest way through, then on to the target. This applies to the normal plot guide too. The fence radius moved into `GameConfig.Lobby`.
- **The tutorial's arrow stays until you're 6 studs away,** not 12. At 12 it hid before you were within the piles' 10-stud grab reach.
- **`TutorialProgress` was removed** from the remotes: the step comes from saved progress.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection, walking with `Humanoid:MoveTo` along the arrow and pressing E for real):**
- **New player (Done when):**
  - After `resetProfile` and a rejoin, the prompt read "Grab seeds at an acorn pile" and the arrow led to the nearest pile (about 1 s away).
  - E grabbed a seed; the prompt switched to "Plant them in a glowing tile", and the arrow led through the gate at 60° to the open row.
  - E planted the first seed.
  - Walking took **9.7 s in all**, plus the two key presses, against the 20 s target. The prompt disappeared after that first plant.
- **Later steps:**
  - After 3 plants: "Get stronger to carry more", with the arrow heading to the 1x pad (by way of a gate).
  - With Strength 10 and 60 coins: "Spend coins on upgrades", toward the shop ring.
  - After buying an upgrade the prompts stopped, and the arrow went back to leading to plots.
- **Free gift:**
  - With a fresh profile, the popup appeared on its own after 90 s of play (screenshot), and the "Free gift!" button was visible until claimed.
  - Claiming added exactly 500 coins; five more claims, fired straight at the remote, added nothing. The button and popup then went away.
- StyLua, Selene and luau-lsp pass.

**Not verified:**
- The Favorite button's Roblox prompt (Studio may not show it).
- A phone (touch controls, where the prompt line sits).
- A returning player mid-tutorial. It follows from the same saved counters, since the step is recomputed from them.

## Changes after M10 (2026-10-06)

- **Trees don't collide any more:** players run straight through trunks; they used to turn solid at full grown. The camera still sees trunks, which fade when they're in the way. The Ancient Tree's trunk stays solid. Verified: none of 14 full-grown trunks were solid, and a character ran from one side of a trunk to the other.

## Follow-up: story intro and fixed steps (2026-10-07)

The user asked for a story cutscene and a set order: pick up → plant → train → pick up more → plant → buy from the shop → complete. The step is now saved (`TutorialStep`, moved on by `TutorialService`) instead of worked out from stats, so the order is fixed. A skippable intro plays first. See the "Tutorial with a story intro" section in [docs/HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md).
