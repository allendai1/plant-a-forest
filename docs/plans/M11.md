The art pass was "M12, after the MVP". It moves ahead of the performance pass (now M12), so performance is measured on the finished look (decided 2026-10-06).

## 1. Goal

Make the whole map match the two style references (`ChatGPT Image Oct 6, 2026, 02_07_57 PM.png` for the map, `Magical Treehouse Forest Plaza.png` for the lobby):
- a floating island with rocky cliffs, waterfalls and small floating islands around it
- clouds and a sunny sky
- tiles that read clearly as dirt plots that turn grassy when planted
- the empty parts of the plaza filled
- real animal models for the break
- more variety in the trees

Done when: screenshots from the spawn, from above and from the forest's edge look like the references, everything still works, and Studio still runs at 60 fps with a full forest.

## What changed from the plan

- **`tools/build_lobby.luau` is now `tools/build_map.luau`** and builds the island, waterfalls, floating islands, bridges, clouds, Lighting and the plaza decorations as well as the lobby.
- **The walk floor is part of the island asset** (`Island_Walk`, renamed `WalkFloor` and tagged so ForestService's raycasts skip it), just under the tile tops.
- **`ForestFloor` stays,** invisible and walk-through, because ForestService finds each plot's height by raycasting onto it.
- **The template's Baseplate is deleted:** made invisible, its grid texture still showed under the island.
- **Lighter haze** than first built: Atmosphere Density 0.1, Offset 0.05, Haze 0.3, Glare 0.1 (0.28/0.18/1 hid the far side of the map).
- **The decorations moved and grew:**
  - At 210°, 270° and 330° they sat right behind seed racks, and the FREE GIFT sign overlapped SEEDS.
  - They're now in the gaps between a rack and a trail: the gift board at 225°, the fountain at 285°, the flower garden at 345°.
  - They're scaled 1.6x, and the gift ring's touch zone follows the board's glowing ring.
- **The waterfall mist** uses Roblox's soft smoke texture; the default sparkle looked like a small sun.
- **Two bugs found while testing:**
  - **Everything in the first art upload came in at 0.78 scale** (island, tiles, animals). The far cloud ring was wider than Roblox's 2048-stud mesh limit, so the importer shrank the whole file to fit. Now the ring is four quarter meshes, and the clouds upload as their own FBX.
  - **The waterfalls shot sideways into the sky:** the bottom attachment's `WorldPosition` was set before it had a parent, which makes it a local offset.
