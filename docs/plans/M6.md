# M6. Forest complete: cutscene, break and the Ancient Spring

Status: **done 2026-10-06.**

## 1. Goal

From `docs/MVP_PLAN.md` (M6, "Forest Awakening"), as changed by your answers: when the bar fills, everyone online gets the forest reward and a short skippable cutscene shows the Ancient Tree finishing. Then comes a 2-minute break where the Ancient Spring trains Strength and Speed at a random multiplier (higher ones are rarer), and anyone can pay Robux to extend the break for the whole server. Then a white flash and a new forest (MVP: tier 1 again), with the Ancient Tree back to a sapling. Stronger training stations unlock from forests completed.

Plus a Studio-only test panel to fill the open row, the section or the whole forest with one click.

Done when: filling the bar in a test (with the test panel) plays the cutscene and the break, grants the reward to everyone online, and resets the grid.

## 4. Studio test panel

A small panel on the right edge of the screen, **only in Studio** (live servers don't even have the `DevCommand` remote it uses):

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fill row | Fills the open row; the row moment plays and the next row opens |
| Fill section | Fills every remaining row of the open section; the section moment plays and the next section opens |
| Fill forest | Fills every plot; the forest completes |
| Leave 1 tree (toggle) | The three fills above leave one empty plot, so you can plant the last tree yourself and watch the moment |
| +50 seeds | Adds 50 carried seeds |
| Extend break | Adds 60 s to the break, like a purchase |
| End break | Skips the rest of the break and resets the forest |

A "–" button collapses it.

## 5. Remotes and data

- **`AwakeningReward`** (S→C, one player): `{ coins: number }`, sent to each player online when the forest completes. The client shows the message when its cutscene ends.
- **`ForestReset` dropped.** There are no new client→server remotes. The extend button uses Roblox's own purchase prompt, and the server only acts on the receipt Roblox sends it. The test panel uses the Studio-only `DevCommand`.
- **Attributes on `ForestState`:** `AwakeningEndsAt` (planned in ARCHITECTURE), `SpringMultiplier` (new; 0 when the spring is closed), and `ExtendedBy` (new: the name of the last player who extended, for the message). **On each player:** `InSpring` (new). `SeedsThisForest` and `CoinBoostEndsAt` aren't needed any more.
- **Saved data:** none yet. `Coins` and `ForestsCompleted` stay in memory until M8. Handled PurchaseIds are kept in server memory, which is enough for a purchase that only affects the current server.
- **For you:** create 6 developer products on the Creator Dashboard (e.g. "Extend the break" at 10, 20, 40, 80, 160 and 320 Robux) and send me their ids, cheapest first.

## 6. Decisions

1. **Spring odds** (rolled once per break for the whole server): 2x 75%, 3x 15%, 5x 10%, averaging about 2.45x, or about +1,200 Strength and Speed over a 2-minute break.
2. **Extensions:** +60 s each, up to 8 minutes of break in all. The price doubles with each extension in a break (one developer product per step).

## What changed from the plan

- **Movement isn't locked during the cutscene.** This place has no `PlayerModule` to disable (Roblox's newer default controls). Waiting for it delayed the cutscene by 5 seconds, so the lock was dropped. The camera is scripted for those 8 seconds anyway.
- **The last section's own moment shows no banner** when it completes the whole forest, so "Section 6 of 6 complete!" doesn't overwrite "Forest complete! +500 coins". Its wave still plays under the cutscene.
- **The spring is open during the cutscene too** (the break timer covers both), so a player already standing in the circle starts training at once.
- **Extensions bought between breaks** (or past the cap) are kept in server memory for the next break, which shows them as "Someone extended the break".
- **Spreadsheet:** a 120 s break plus an 8 s cutscene; 500 coins and +1 forest to everyone online; spring odds 2x 75% / 3x 15% / 5x 10%; extensions of +60 s up to 8 minutes. `ForestCreditSeeds` and `AwakeningBoost` are unused for now.

**Verified in Studio (one player, through the Studio connection, with real mouse clicks on the test panel):**
- **Test panel:** "Fill row" filled the open row (560 seeds) and opened the next. With "Leave 1 tree" on, "Fill section" left exactly one plot of the section and "Fill forest" left exactly one in the whole forest. "+50 seeds", "Extend break" and "End break" reached the server. Two early clicks didn't, while the mouse was elsewhere (probably activity in the Studio window); the same clicks worked on retry.
- **Forest complete by planting the last tree by hand:** Coins 0 → 540 (40 for the seeds plus the 500 reward), `ForestsCompleted` 0 → 1, the break timer at 128 s (8 + 120) and the spring rolled.
- **Cutscene:** started 0.01 s after the break began, with the HUD hidden and the Skip button showing; the screenshot shows the camera circling the glowing, blooming tree with its light beam. Skip returned the camera at once and brought the HUD back. Then "Forest complete! +500 coins" and "The Ancient Spring is 2x! Stand in the glowing circle." showed, in that order.
- **Spring:** inside the rune circle at 2x, 2 seconds gave +16 Strength and +16 Speed (8 reps × 2) and showed the HUD label; outside, nothing. 10,000 rolls of the odds came out 75.0% / 15.3% / 9.7%, and the boundaries are exact.
- **Extensions:** an extension bought before the break was applied when it started. Seven more requests stopped at 6 extensions with the break capped at 8 minutes, the button hid at the cap, and the "extended the break by 1:00!" message showed.
- **Break performance:** 61 fps with all 594 trees swaying and the animals out; one sway step for all 1,188 tree parts takes 0.25 ms. One earlier reading of 16 fps came from Studio being slowed by something outside the game: frames took 66 ms but rendering only 5 ms, and the same scene measured 61 fps later.
- **Reset:** the white flash played, then every plot was empty (no trees, no animals, no beam). `Seeds` was 0, the rack section's outer row was open again (14 rims), the Ancient Tree was back to a 16-stud sapling and the spring was closed. A second forest gave `ForestsCompleted` 2 and another +500 coins.
- StyLua, Selene and luau-lsp pass. All 22 scripts in Studio match the disk byte for byte.
