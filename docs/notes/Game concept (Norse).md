# Game concept: the Norse World Tree (proposal, 2026-10-09)

A proposal from the 2026-10-09 session, not decided yet. Working title: **Grow the World Tree** (Yggdrasil stays in the story; it's hard to spell into a search bar). It keeps every mechanic that exists and adds the hooks the current game is missing.

## The pitch in one line

Ragnarok burned the nine realms and Yggdrasil is dying. You're a young Viking carrying the World Tree's seeds into each burned realm; every seed brings the land back, and Yggdrasil grows until you can climb it to Valhalla.

## What makes it stick (the four hooks)

1. **Every seed feels good:** ash turns green under your feet, a coin pops, the tree bounces, the pitch rises. You do this thousands of times, so it has to be the juiciest thing in the game.
2. **A big moment every few minutes:** a ring completes, then the realm is restored (the Awakening), then everyone climbs Yggdrasil to Valhalla, then the Bifrost carries the server to a new realm.
3. **There's always a next goal you can see:** the next training stone down the street, the next rank on your nameplate, the next rune for your collection, the next Ragnarok.
4. **A reason to come back tomorrow:** your own sapling grew while you were away, your daily blessing streak, new realms each update.

## The loops

**Seconds: the trip** (exists). Grab seeds at the seed pile; walk into the burned field; each seed pays coins, grows its tree a step and turns ash to living ground; walk back.

**Minutes: getting stronger** (exists, reskinned). The Viking training ground: lifting stones (Strength, how much you carry) and a running track (Speed), in a row from 1x by the garden to 100x down the street. The trader sells Bulk Pickup, Bulk Plant and Planting Range.

**Ten minutes: restoring a realm** (exists). The server fills the realm's rings (rounds: 3 rings, then 6, then all 9). When it's restored, the Awakening plays, everyone who helped gets coins and +1 realm restored, and the break begins: climb Yggdrasil to **Valhalla**, where Odin's warriors train every day, for a 2x/3x/5x training boost. Then the **Bifrost** takes the server to the next realm.

**Days: the long game** (new):
- **Ragnarok (rebirth):** reset your Strength and Speed for a permanent multiplier on everything you gain. It fits the myth (the world ends and is reborn), and rebirths are the proven long-term engine of Roblox incremental games.
- **Runes:** each tree you finish has a small chance to drop a rune stone (a different set per realm). A rune collection board shows what you have; each rune is a small permanent bonus, and a full realm set a bigger one. Rare drops are announced to the server.
- **Your sapling of Yggdrasil:** a small tree of your own in the village that grows while you're offline and holds coins for you (up to about a day's worth), collected with one big sweep when you come back ("Your sapling grew while you were away!").
- **Daily blessing from Odin:** a 7-day streak reward, the 7th day special.
- Viking ranks on the nameplate (Thrall, Karl, Warrior, Jarl, up to Einherjar), quests, leaderboards, the global counter of trees regrown.

## The first ten minutes (the target)

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | You spawn facing Yggdrasil, grey and dying, with the burned field beyond it; one arrow to the seed pile |
| 0:15 | First seeds; first tile turns green |
| 0:45 | First tree grown: a celebration and +100 coins |
| 1:30 | Train on the 1x stones next to the garden |
| 2:30 | First upgrade at the trader |
| 6-8 min | The realm is restored (round 1 is 63 trees): the Awakening, the reward, the 2x stones unlock |
| 8-10 min | The climb to Valhalla, then the Bifrost to the next realm |

The tutorial shrinks to about four steps (pick up, plant, train, done); the shop is taught by a pulse when you can afford it. Field data says hit tutorials run under a minute, and our training step loses a third of new players.

## Realms (worlds)

Midgard (today's Sherwood), Alfheim (today's Kyoto, glowing blossoms), Vanaheim (today's Smoky Mountains, golden autumn); later one per update: Jotunheim (snowy giant pines), Svartalfheim (giant mushrooms), Muspelheim (ember trees), Niflheim (ice crystals), Helheim (ghostly willows, a Halloween event). Yggdrasil is the same tree in every realm, tinted per realm.

## Look

Players are Vikings, all the same (horned helmet, braided beard, fur cloak), replacing the gnome. Paid looks become Norse heroes (the Forest Ranger becomes a Shieldmaiden or Berserker; Lord of the Forest a Jarl). The rectangular town becomes a Viking village with longhouses.

## Money (fits what exists)

Valhalla time (today's break extension), training boosts, the Bifrost realm pick (today's world pick), hero looks, a rune-luck boost later. The game stays fully playable without paying.

## What it costs, by priority

1. Norse reskin and story (names, Viking look, runes on the tree, the Bifrost, a short Ragnarok intro, a shorter tutorial): about 2 days.
2. Ragnarok rebirth: about 1 day plus balancing.
3. Runes and the collection board: about 1-2 days.
4. Your sapling (offline coins) and the daily blessing streak: about 1 day.
5. The Viking village town: about 2 days.
6. New realms: 1-2 days of art each, one per update.

## Open decisions

The title; Vikings instead of gnomes; whether Ragnarok, runes and the sapling are all wanted, and in what order.
