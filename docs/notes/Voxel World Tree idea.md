# Voxel World Tree idea (2026-10-09)

After the [Trojan Horse idea](Trojan%20Horse%20idea.md) (the user: a horse is too small for a Build the Pyramid-style game; it can't keep growing): **Build the World Tree**, a voxel Yggdrasil built block by block that can always grow higher. Mythology, trees, and the [Norse concept](Game%20concept%20(Norse).md)'s story. Not decided; the runner-up idea is the Great Green Wall (the real tree wall across Africa: the Great Wall formula with our planting game).

## The model

`Workspace.WorldTreePreview`, 2,200 studs east of the island, look-only: 6-stud blocks, 2,708 bark and 4,548 leaf blocks (only the outer shell), 480 studs tall and about 300 across. Root flare and six roots, a trunk narrowing from 14 to 8 blocks across, six branches angling up with leaf clusters, and a two-part crown. A standard player stands near the base for scale. Made by a script from simple shapes; saved with the place if you save; delete the folder to remove it.

## How building it could work (proposal)

- Layer by layer from the roots up: the current layer's empty slots glow, the next opens when it's full; bark for the trunk and branches, leaves for the crowns.
- It never has to end: the trunk keeps rising and new branches and crowns are added higher up, so a server's tree height is its score; realms can hang on branches (Midgard low, Asgard and Valhalla near the top).
- You stand on what you built: the trunk's top layer and the branches are the work area, reached by a stair around the trunk.
- Blocks come from the forest around it (grown trees give wood and leaves), keeping capacity and speed.
