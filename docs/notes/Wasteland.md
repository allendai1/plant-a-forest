# Wasteland (2026-10-09)

The user's pick from the "ideas for Plant Trees!" brainstorm: the forest area starts as dry wasteland and planting brings it back to life. The hub always stays green.

- **Now (colors only):** an unplanted tile is dry, dusty tan ground with a dry grass edge; its first acorn turns it green (moss, tufts, flowers, the old planted look). Rings outside the round look the same; the open tiles are marked by their glowing rim. Colors: `OPEN_COLOR` and `DRY_BORDER_COLOR` at the top of ForestController (the brown dirt was 150, 103, 64).
- A dusty tan on purpose: a slate grey for unopened tiles was tried on 2026-10-07 and looked ugly to the user.
- **Every forest starts as wasteland again** after the break; the Forest Awakening is the moment the land is healed.
- **Later, if it needs more:** dead props on unplanted tiles (stumps, dry bushes, rocks; about half a day plus a phone frame-rate check), and the world's colors starting washed out and brightening as the Forest Bar fills.
