# Trojan Horse idea (2026-10-09)

A new-game idea from the 2026-10-09 brainstorm (after the Norse concept, a beaver dam that turned out to be taken, and a chopping game): **Build the Trojan Horse**, a voxel horse built block by block like Build the Pyramid, with the forest as the source of the wood. Not decided.

## How it would work

- **The horse is a voxel model** (the user's pick over wedges or a sculpted mesh): wooden blocks with Roblox's WoodPlanks material, colored per part (light body, dark mane, tail and hooves, eyes), on a wheeled platform.
- **Built layer by layer from the hooves up:** the current layer's empty slots glow, the next layer opens when it's full; a faint outline of the finished horse shows from the start. Only the shell is built (the myth's horse was hollow).
- **Pacing from the shape:** small leg layers (fast first wins), the big belly (the slow middle), the shrinking neck and head (a fast finish).
- **Scaffold stairs** beside it; the horse's back is walkable once built.
- **The finale:** everyone climbs into the belly, the doors close, the horse rolls to the gates of Troy.
- **Wood from the forest:** chop grown trees for planks, replant saplings.
- **Rounds:** a wooden pony first (~60 studs, ~250 blocks), then a horse (~100 studs, ~700), then the full Trojan Horse. Planks per block scale with the player count, like seeds per tree.
- Carry, place, training (Strength to capacity, Speed to walk speed), upgrades and analytics carry over from Plant Trees!.

## The model

`Workspace.TrojanHorsePreview`, 2,200 studs north of the island, look-only: 1,368 shell blocks of 5 studs; 150 studs long, 155 tall with the wheels, 55 wide; a standard player stands by the platform for scale. Made by a script from simple shapes (ellipsoids and capsules), not a Blender model yet. Saved with the place if you save; delete the folder to remove it.

Open: whether to make this the game, the setting (a Greek beach camp, Troy's walls), and checking the title isn't taken on Roblox.
